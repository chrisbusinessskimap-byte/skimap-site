Account

Delete your account.

You can delete your Ski Maps account at any time, from the app or by email. Here is how, how long it takes and what is deleted.

From the app

If you still have the app on your phone, this is the quickest way:

  1. Open Ski Maps.
  2. [DA CONFERMARE: percorso esatto nell’app, per esempio Impostazioni → Account → Elimina account]
  3. Confirm. [DA CONFERMARE: l’app chiede una conferma, la password o un nuovo accesso con Google? l’eliminazione è immediata?]

If you no longer have the app

Write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com with the subject “Delete account”. Write from the email address of the account, so that we can recognise it. [DA CONFERMARE: come verifichiamo che l’account è tuo: basta l’email? serve l’ID utente? cosa si fa se hai usato l’accesso con Google?]

How long it takes

We reply within one month, as for other privacy requests. [DA CONFERMARE: entro quanti giorni l’account viene cancellato, dall’app e per email? il termine di un mese vale anche qui?]

What is deleted

[DA CONFERMARE: elenco preciso dei dati cancellati con l’account: email, ID utente, collegamento con l’accesso Google, altro]

What stays, and for how long

[DA CONFERMARE: dati che restano dopo l’eliminazione (copie di sicurezza, obblighi di legge) e per quanto tempo]

Some information is not linked to your account and deletes itself when it expires: closed-piste reports, lift waiting times and leg times (see How long data is kept in the privacy notice). [DA CONFERMARE: vale ancora se l’utente aveva un account?] On the phone, “Delete my data” in Settings deletes the random diagnostics code and the data still waiting to be sent.

Your Google account

If you signed in with Google, deleting your Ski Maps account does not delete your Google account. To remove Ski Maps’ permission to sign in, use the security settings of your Google account.

Questions

For any doubt write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com. The rest about data processing is in the privacy notice.
