Your data

Delete your data.

This version of Ski Maps has no account to delete. Here is how to delete what stays on your phone, how to ask for what reached the server to be deleted, and when it is deleted automatically.

On your phone

Open Ski Maps, go to Settings and tap “Delete my data”. The app removes from the phone the random code, the events still waiting to be sent, the saved votes, the times not yet sent and the anonymous account used for reports. On restart you are asked again what you prefer.

On the server

What has already reached the server stays there until it expires, then it is deleted automatically: every night whatever has expired is deleted.

  • Diagnostics events: 12 months.
  • Votes on runs: 30 days.
  • Lift waiting times: 60 days.
  • Leg times: 12 months.
  • The anonymous account behind reports, waiting times and leg times: 12 months without use.

Deleting diagnostics sooner

To find out which diagnostics events exist, or to have them deleted before they expire, write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com with your random code: you will get an answer within a month. You will find the code in Settings: note it down before you tap “Delete my data”, which deletes it.

Reports, waiting times and leg times

They are not linked to the diagnostics code, and we have no way of telling which ones are yours: they are deleted automatically when they expire. To stop sending more: times are switched off with “Automatic contributions” in Settings, and reports are only sent when you vote.

The website waiting list

If you left your email in the “Notify me at launch” form, write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Questions

For any question write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com. Everything else about how data is handled is in the privacy notice. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
