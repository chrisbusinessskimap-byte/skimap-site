How it works

One route.
The whole tour.

The best tours run from one ski area to the next. Ski Maps keeps them together in a single route, including the buses and trains that connect them.

Tours across ski areas

The problem

Stuck halfway round

Between ski areas it is not always clear which piste, lift or bus takes you onward or back.

With Ski Maps

You always know the next step

From where you are to where you want to be: pistes, lifts, buses and trains, one after another.

In three steps

Three steps.
Then ski.

  1. 1Step 1

    Choose where you want to go

    Pick the point on the map where you want to end up: a lift, a mountain hut, the village.

  2. 2Step 2

    Choose your level

    Beginner, intermediate or advanced: the route follows your level.

  3. 3Step 3

    Follow the directions

    Piste by piste and lift by lift, with the piste name and colour and voice guidance. Your phone can stay in your pocket.

Features

What it does,
on the snow.

Features already tested on the phone.

  • Routes by level

    Beginner, intermediate or advanced: you choose, the route adapts.

  • Lift closures and hours

    The app knows lift closures and opening hours and tells you whether you can get back in time.

  • Voice guidance

    Directions are spoken while you ski.

  • Phone in your pocket

    Navigation keeps going with your phone in your pocket.

  • Works offline

    Maps and routes can be downloaded, so you can use them with no signal.

  • Piste name and colour

    Every direction gives the name of the piste and its colour.

  • In five languages

    Italian, English, German, French and Spanish.
