The waiting list sign-up

If you leave your email in the “Notify me at launch” form on this website, your mail app opens with a message ready for Ski Maps’ mailbox: it is only sent if you send it, like a normal email from your address. It is used for one thing only: letting you know when Ski Maps is out. No newsletter, no passing on to third parties, and signing up does not create an account. To have it deleted, just reply to the address above.

This website

This website (skimaps.cc) does not use cookies and has no analytics or tracking tools. Fonts and scripts are served by the site itself, not by other sites. The site is published with GitHub Pages: like any service that hosts a website, it sees your IP address and the technical details of the request.
