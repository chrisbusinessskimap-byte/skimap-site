Delete the account from the app

Open You → Account → Delete account, then tap “Delete” to confirm. The account is deleted from the server straight away: the email address, the Google link if you used one, the synced preferences (level, map, voice guidance) and the contributions sent today. The data covered by “Delete my data” is also removed from the phone. You need a connection.

Without the app

If you no longer have the phone or have uninstalled the app, write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject “Delete account”: that is what tells us the account is yours. We delete it and confirm by email, within a month.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: the account (email, creation and last-sign-in dates, Google identity) and the synced preferences.
  • Closed-run reports, waiting times and leg times are not linked to the account: they are saved under a pseudonym that changes every day, so those from earlier days can no longer be traced back to you. Those sent today are deleted together with the account; the rest is deleted automatically when it expires.
  • Diagnostics, if you had them on, are tied to the installation code and not to the account: they are deleted automatically after 12 months, or sooner if you write to us with the code you find in the app, under Settings.
  • An account you do not use for 24 months is deleted automatically.

On your phone

Open Ski Maps, go to Settings and tap “Delete my data”. The app removes from the phone the random code, the events still waiting to be sent, the saved votes, the times not yet sent, the paused navigation and the offline route maps. If you have no account, as soon as there is a connection it also asks the server to delete the anonymous identity and the votes, waiting times and leg times sent today; if you have an account, you are signed out, but the account stays until you delete it as explained above. On restart you are asked again what you prefer.

On the server

What has already reached the server stays there until it expires, then it is deleted automatically: every night whatever has expired is deleted.

  • Diagnostics events: 12 months.
  • Votes on runs: 30 days.
  • Lift waiting times: 60 days.
  • Leg times: 12 months.
  • The anonymous identity: 12 months without use.
  • The account: 24 months without use, together with the synced preferences.

Deleting diagnostics sooner

To find out which diagnostics events exist, or to have them deleted before they expire, write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com with your random code: you will get an answer within a month. You will find the code in Settings: note it down before you tap “Delete my data”, which deletes it.

Reports, waiting times and leg times

They are linked neither to the diagnostics code nor, from the next day on, to any account: we have no way of telling which ones are yours, and they are deleted automatically when they expire. To stop sending more: times are switched off with “Automatic contributions” in Settings, and reports are only sent when you vote.

The website waiting list

If you left your email in the “Notify me at launch” form, write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Questions

For any question write to chris.business.skimap@gmail.com. Everything else about how data is handled is in the privacy notice. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
